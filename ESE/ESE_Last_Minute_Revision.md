## 0.1 Formula and framework card

### Financial formulas

| Formula | Use |
|---|---|
| **Contribution per unit = Price − Variable cost** | Money each unit contributes to fixed costs |
| **BEP (units) = Fixed cost ÷ Contribution per unit** | Break-even volume |
| **BEP (sales) = Fixed cost ÷ Contribution margin ratio** | Break-even revenue |
| **Contribution margin ratio = (Price − VC) ÷ Price** | Margin percentage |
| **Gross margin = (Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue** | Product profitability |
| **CAC = Marketing + sales spend ÷ New customers** | Cost to acquire one customer |
| **LTV = ARPU × Gross margin × Average customer life** (or ARPU × margin ÷ churn) | Value of one customer |
| **LTV : CAC ≥ 3 : 1** | Healthy unit economics rule of thumb |
| **Burn rate = Net cash spent per month** | Cash consumption |
| **Runway (months) = Cash balance ÷ Monthly burn** | Time before cash runs out |
| **ROI = (Gain − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100** | Return on investment |
| **Payback period = Investment ÷ Annual cash inflow** | Years to recover capital |
| **Valuation (revenue multiple) = Revenue × Multiple** | Quick valuation sanity check |
| **Post-money = Pre-money + Investment** | Round maths |
| **Dilution = Investment ÷ Post-money** | New investor’s stake |
| **TAM/SAM/SOM** | Bottom-up: units × price × buyers |

**Worked fan example:** price ₹2,500, BOM ₹1,400 ⇒ contribution ₹1,100. Fixed cost ₹22,00,000 ⇒ BEP = 22,00,000 ÷ 1,100 = **2,000 fans/year**.

### Funding stages

| Stage | What the money is for | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| **Bootstrapping** | Prototype, first customers | Founders, savings, friends/family |
| **Pre-seed** | Idea → concrete plan; team | Angels, accelerators, grants |
| **Seed** | Launch first product; early traction | Angels, seed VCs, incubators |
| **Series A** | Repeatable model; scale sales/team | VCs |
| **Series B/C** | Market expansion, acquisitions | Larger VCs, growth funds |
| **Pre-IPO / IPO** | Public markets, liquidity | Investment banks, public investors |

### Frameworks

- **Business Model Canvas:** Key Partners · Key Activities · Key Resources · Value Proposition · Customer Relationships · Channels · Customer Segments · Cost Structure · Revenue Streams.
- **TELOS feasibility:** Technical · Economic · Legal · Operational · Scheduling.
- **STP:** Segment → Target → Position. Positioning statement: *For [target], [brand] is the [category] that [benefit] because [proof].*
- **4Ps:** Product · Price · Place · Promotion.
- **Lean Startup:** Build → Measure → Learn; MVP; validated learning; pivot.
- **Design thinking:** Empathise → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test.
- **Pitch:** Problem → Solution → Market → Business model → Traction → Team → Ask.

## 1. Unit 1 — Entrepreneurial Ecosystem

**Most repeated:** entrepreneurship vs startup ★★; local vs global ★★; incubators/accelerators ★★; Indian case study ★.

### Definitions

| Term | One-line definition |
|---|---|
| **Entrepreneurship** | Process of identifying an opportunity, organising resources and bearing risk to create value |
| **Startup** | New venture built around an innovation, designed for rapid scalable growth under uncertainty |
| **Entrepreneur** | The person who spots the opportunity and organises the venture |
| **Incubator** | Early-stage support over 1–5 years; space, labs, mentorship; little/no equity |
| **Accelerator** | Fixed 3–6 month cohort for MVP-stage startups; seed + mentorship + Demo Day; 5–10% equity |
| **Ecosystem** | Interconnected founders, capital, academia, government, support organisations and culture |

### Entrepreneurship vs startup (5-mark table)

| Point | Entrepreneurship | Startup |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Broad, any size | Innovative, scalable |
| Growth | Steady/linear | Rapid/exponential |
| Innovation | May be incremental | Central |
| Funding | Savings, family, bank | Angels, VC, accelerators |
| Risk | Moderate–high | High uncertainty |
| Success | Sustainable profit | Traction, scale, exit |
| Example | Kirana, consultancy | Zomato, Ather |

### Role in the economy (6 points)

**Jobs → Innovation → GDP → Exports → Regional balance → Social impact.** Add resilience and policy support. Indian examples: Zomato/Swiggy gig jobs; UPI fintech; Zoho/Freshworks exports; Indore/Coimbatore/Jaipur.

### Local vs global (6–8 rows)

Market · Culture · Resources/capital · Policy · Talent · Competition · Growth goals · Risk · Support ecosystem.
Example: **Flipkart** — CoD + Ekart for India; **Ather** — Bengaluru beachhead first.

### Incubator vs accelerator (7 rows)

Stage · Duration · Objective · Equity · Structure · Host · Example. SINE/T-Hub vs Y Combinator (Airbnb, Stripe, Razorpay).

### Indian success case — Zomato’s 7 factors

Real problem → simple app → logistics → customer experience/trust → technology/data → funding → pivots/resilience. Ola = capital-led scaling; BYJU’S = cautionary (growth over unit economics/governance).

## 2. Unit 2 — Startup Mindset

**Most repeated:** feasibility ★★★; innovation ★★★; mindset ★★; segmentation ★★; creativity ★★.

### Mindset traits (pick any 3–6)

**Opportunity orientation · calculated risk · bias for action · growth mindset · resourcefulness · ownership.**
Managerial vs entrepreneurial: optimise existing resources vs pursue opportunity beyond resources; avoid failure vs treat failure as data; wait for complete information vs experiment; salary/security vs equity/impact; causal vs effectual logic.

### Market analysis and segmentation

- **TAM** = everyone; **SAM** = your channels can serve; **SOM** = realistic 2–3 years.
- **Segmentation bases:** demographic, geographic, psychographic, behavioural.
- **STP:** segment → target (beachhead) → position.
- Benefits: sharper message, lower CAC, right customers, loyalty, differentiation, niche discovery.
- Example: Ather (premium urban professionals, Bengaluru first); Nike (perform/train/lifestyle); Atomberg (energy-conscious households).

### Feasibility — TELOS

**T**echnical (can we build it?), **E**conomic (do the numbers work?), **L**egal (are we allowed?), **O**perational (can we run it?), **S**cheduling (can we do it in time?).
Add market, financial, environmental/social. Steps: define concept → test dimensions → deal-breakers → go/pivot/stop → revisit.
Example: **Agnikul** — engine test first, 3D-printing economics, IN-SPACe, launchpad, milestone funding.

### Innovation

Invention = new; innovation = new + adopted + value. Types: **product, process, business model, service, social**; incremental/radical/disruptive.
Why it helps: differentiation, value, scalability, sustainability, disruption, barriers to imitation.
Example: Netflix (subscription, no late fees, streaming); Tesla (product + process + model); Atomberg (BLDC); Agnikul (3D-printed engine).

### Creativity

**Novelty × usefulness.** Helps problem solving, differentiation, adaptability, innovation, motivation, cost-effective resource use.
Blockers: premature judgement, fear, functional fixedness, expert bias, homogeneous teams, no slack.
Boosters: psychological safety, cross-pollination, constraints, exposure, timeboxes.
Tools: brainstorming, brainwriting 6-3-5, SCAMPER, mind map, design thinking, observation.
Example: 3M Post-it (failed adhesive + 15% time); Phool (temple waste).

### Opportunity recognition

Idea → opportunity only if **desirable, feasible, viable**. Sources: pain points, inefficiencies, waste, regulation, technology, trends. Process: perception → discovery → formation → evaluation.

## 3. Unit 3 — Business Model Development

**Most repeated:** HRM ★★★; financial projections ★★; SCM ★★; 4Ps/operations/Lean Startup ★ (F25).

### Business Model Canvas (9 blocks)

Key Partners · Key Activities · Key Resources · Value Proposition · Customer Relationships · Channels · Customer Segments · Cost Structure · Revenue Streams.
Order to fill: Segments → Value Proposition → Channels → Revenue → Activities/Resources/Partners → Costs.
Example: Atomberg — “65% lower power bill”, D2C + marketplaces, BOM + marketing costs, SINE incubation.

### Prototype / MVP types

Paper/CAD → PoC → 3D-printed works-like → pilot/field → pre-order/landing page.
MVP is a learning instrument, not a bad product. Example: Agnikul’s Agnilet engine before the rocket.

### Lean Startup

**Build → Measure → Learn.** Test the riskiest assumption; measure real behaviour; validated learning; persevere/pivot.
Pivots: customer segment, zoom-in, channel, technology.
Example: Airbnb (air mattresses → photos/reviews); Zomato (discovery → delivery → quick commerce).

### Finance

Revenue bottom-up (units × price), costs (fixed vs variable), contribution, BEP, cash flow, budget.
**BEP = FC ÷ (P − VC).** Fan: (2,500 − 1,400) = 1,100; 22,00,000 ÷ 1,100 = 2,000.
Metrics: burn, runway, CAC, LTV (≥3× CAC), unit economics.
Tools: Excel/Sheets, QuickBooks/Xero, Adaptive/PlanGuru.
Importance: planning, decisions, risk, performance, investor confidence.

### Compliance — 6 steps

Stay informed → policies/procedures → training → audits → legal/compliance experts → compliance programme.
India checklist: incorporation/DPIIT, GST/TDS, BIS/ARAI/DGCA, EPF/ESI/POSH, founders’ agreement/vesting, DPDP/e-waste.

### ESG

Benefits: reputation, funding, regulatory readiness, cost savings, talent, differentiation.
Challenges: high initial cost, complex rules, expertise gap, scaling, greenwashing, reduced agility.
Sequence: mandatory first (e-waste, labour, POSH, DPDP), then voluntary differentiation.

### HRM

Functions: recruitment/staffing, training/development, performance, compensation/benefits, employee relations, compliance, workforce planning.
Startup: hire for stage, ESOPs (4-year vesting, 1-year cliff), culture by design, frequent feedback, retention, founder roles.
Productivity: right hire → faster ramp; training → fewer errors; ESOPs → ownership; recognition → engagement.
Examples: Google onboarding, Amazon Career Choice, Adobe Check-In, Starbucks rewards, Salesforce wellness, Microsoft D&I, IBM analytics.

### SCM

Sourcing → production → delivery; flows of materials, information, finance.
Components: transportation, warehousing, customer returns, supplier/inventory, information.
Scaling: ERP, diversify suppliers, optimize logistics, quality systems, make-vs-buy, reverse logistics.
Example: Ather designs battery/software in-house; standard parts from vendors.

### Operations

Process design, capacity, production planning, quality, inventory, supply-chain coordination, maintenance, continuous improvement.
Delivers: cost, quality, speed, reliability, scalability, cash.

### Marketing mix (4Ps)

Product (value proposition, not spec sheet) · Price (value-based vs penetration) · Place (D2C → retail; experience centres) · Promotion (content, community, demos, reviews).
Sequence: one beachhead × one channel × one message, measured by CAC.

### Ownership forms

Sole prop (unlimited) · Partnership (unlimited, joint) · LLP (limited, services) · Pvt Ltd (limited, startup default, ESOPs) · OPC (single founder).

### Growth challenges

Premature scaling, quality slips, cash squeeze, founder bottleneck, culture dilution, key-person/supplier dependency. Fix: scale after retention, quality systems, runway discipline, delegation, documented culture, dual sourcing.

## 4. Unit 4 — Innovation and IP

**Most repeated:** patents/patent search ★★; IP role/strategies ★★ (R25 + F25).

### Five IP instruments (India)

| Tool | Protects | Term |
|---|---|---|
| **Patent** | Function — novel, inventive, industrially applicable | 20 years from filing |
| **Trademark** | Identity — name, logo, sound, shape | 10 years, renewable forever |
| **Copyright** | Expression — code, content, drawings | Life + 60 years |
| **Design** | Form/appearance | 10 + 5 years |
| **Trade secret** | Confidential know-how | As long as secret |

### Patents

Tests: **novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability**. Exclusions: discoveries, math methods, business methods as such, traditional knowledge.
Filing: search → provisional (12 months) → complete → publication (18 months) → examination/FER → grant → renew.
Purpose: protect, recoup R&D, disclose knowledge, signal investors, license.
Example: Agnikul single-piece 3D-printed engine; Ather motor/controller.

### Patent search

Purposes: patentability, freedom to operate, design-around, competitor intelligence, cost saving, claim drafting.
Databases: InPASS, Google Patents, PATENTSCOPE, Espacenet, Lens/USPTO.
6 steps: define → keywords → classification (CPC/IPC) → citations → claims → document/decide.

### Trademarks

Benefits: recognition/loyalty, differentiation, legal protection, brand equity, marketing efficiency, trust, licensing.
Example: Ather/Atomberg brand; Nike Swoosh; McDonald’s arches.

### IP strategy

Patent the visible function; keep process know-how secret; trademark the brand; design the form; copyright the code/content; PCT selectively; DPIIT/SIPP rebates; NDAs + IP assignment.
Six mistakes: self-disclosure, unclear founder/institute IP, no assignment clauses, wrong geography, filing everything, ignoring others’ IP.

### Technology venture

Problem → enabling technology → product → validation → scale.
Deep-tech (Agnikul, E-Spin, Log9) vs tech-enabled (Swiggy/Zomato, D2C).
Examples: 3D printing → Agnilet; app + GPS + payments → Swiggy/Zomato; flower recycling → Phool.

### Ethics

Safety/testing honesty, data privacy, AI fairness, dual use, environment, honest claims/IP respect.

## 5. Unit 5 — Fundraising

**Most repeated:** angel investors/angel vs VC ★★★; pitch ★★; funding options/role ★★.

### Funding routes (10)

Bootstrapping · friends/family · bank/NBFC (Stand-Up India, CGTMSE) · government schemes (Startup India, MUDRA, SIDBI) · angels · VC · crowdfunding · incubators/accelerators · corporate/CVC · competitions/grants · revenue-based financing.

### Role of funding

Runway, product, GTM, expansion, shocks, credibility — **not** a substitute for product–market fit. Example: Ola (Series A $5M Tiger Global 2012; Series D ~$210M SoftBank 2014; $1.1B 2017; Ola Electric $200M at ~$5B).

### Angel vs VC (10 rows)

| Point | Angel | VC |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Personal wealth | Pooled LP money |
| Size | Smaller | Larger |
| Stage | Early/seed | Later |
| Involvement | Hands-on | Board-level |
| Decision | Individual | Investment committee |
| Focus | Diverse | Sector funds |
| Risk | Higher tolerance | Portfolio-managed |
| Due diligence | Lighter | Formal |
| Return | Personal/flexible | Fund-level, 10x winners |
| Exit | Variable | 4–6 years, M&A/IPO |

### Angels in India

Types: individual, network, family office, corporate. Networks: Indian Angel Network, Mumbai Angels, Chennai Angels. Names: Sanjay Mehta, Kunal Shah, Anupam Mittal, Ratan Tata, Rajan Anandan, Binny Bansal, Amit Somani.
Role: seed capital, mentorship, networks, credibility, innovation promotion.

### Angel relationship process

Research (AngelList India, LetsVenture, IAN) → nurture (TiE/NASSCOM events, warm intros, updates) → pitch (story, data, edge) → India-market knowledge (localisation, regulation) → team/execution → close and manage.

### Pitch

Elements: problem, solution, business model, market, traction, team, ask.
Importance: first impression, problem/solution clarity, market/scalability, competitive advantage, traction, clear ask, trust.
Delivery: short, problem-first, why you, target market, data, specific ask.

### Crowdfunding

| Platform | Model | Focus | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ketto** | Donation | Medical, social, education | Sharing, mobile app, platform fee |
| **Wishberry** | Reward | Films, music, art, tech | Mentoring, video/marketing support, commission |

Equity crowdfunding is tightly regulated in India — check SEBI rules.

### Financial modelling

Revenue model, expense model, cash flow, scenarios, outputs (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, BEP, runway). Tools: Excel/Sheets, QuickBooks/Xero, Adaptive/PlanGuru.
